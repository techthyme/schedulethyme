Logo Design Prompt:


1. Schedule:
   What is the exact name of the company or brand for which the logo is being created?
2. Industry/Niche:
   What industry does the company operate in? (e.g., tech, food, fashion, finance, education, non-profit, sports, etc.)
   Who is the target audience? (e.g., young adults, families, professionals, luxury consumers, budget-conscious individuals?)
3. Logo Style Preferences:
   What kind of overall style are you aiming for? Choose one or more from below, or describe your own:
   Minimalist: Clean, simple, few elements.
   Modern: Sleek, contemporary, often uses geometric shapes.
   Classic/Timeless: Elegant, traditional, sophisticated.
   Playful/Fun: Whimsical, energetic, often uses bright colors or caricatures.
   Luxurious/Premium: High-end, sophisticated, often uses metallics or elegant fonts.
   Organic/Natural: Earthy, flowing, often uses natural elements.
   Abstract: Uses symbolic shapes or forms without direct representation.
   Bold/Strong: Impactful, memorable, often uses thick lines or strong contrasts.
4. Logo Type Preferences (Optional):
   Do you have a preference for the type of logo?
   Wordmark/Logotype: Just the company name in a stylized font (e.g., Coca-Cola, Google).
   Lettermark/Monogram: An initial or abbreviation in a stylized font (e.g., IBM, NASA).
   Pictorial Mark/Logo Symbol: An icon or graphic that represents the company (e.g., Apple's apple, Twitter's bird).
   Abstract Mark: A unique, abstract graphic that doesn't depict a recognizable object (e.g., Nike's swoosh, Adidas's three stripes).
   Mascot: A character illustration representing the company (e.g., KFC's Colonel Sanders, Michelin Man).
   Combination Mark: A combination of a wordmark and a symbol/icon (e.g., Lacoste, Burger King).
   Emblem: Font inside a symbol or icon, often with a badge-like appearance (e.g., Starbucks, Harley-Davidson).
5. Color Palette Preferences:
   Are there any specific colors you want to use, or avoid?
   Consider the feeling or message you want to convey (e.g., blue for trust, green for nature/growth, red for energy/passion).
   Do you prefer bright, muted, dark, or light colors?
6. Key Elements/Imagery/Concepts:
   Are there any specific objects, symbols, or concepts that you want to incorporate into the logo, or that represent your brand? (e.g., a mountain, a lightbulb, a specific animal, connectivity, growth, speed, innovation).
   Are there any elements you don't want to include?
7. Desired Feeling/Message:
   What feeling or message should the logo convey to someone who sees it? (e.g., trustworthy, innovative, friendly, sophisticated, adventurous, reliable, creative).
8. Inspiration/Examples (Optional):
   Do you have any existing logos you admire, or particular styles you like? You can describe them briefly.
   Example of how you might use the prompt:
   "I need a logo for a new coffee shop.
9. Company/Brand Name: The Daily Grind
10. Industry/Niche: Artisan coffee shop, targeting young professionals and students.
11. Logo Style Preferences: Modern, a bit rustic, but still clean.
12. Logo Type Preferences: Combination mark (text and symbol).
13. Color Palette Preferences: Warm browns, creamy whites, a touch of dark green.
14. Key Elements/Imagery/Concepts: A coffee bean, a steaming mug, or something subtle hinting at morning/start of the day. Maybe a subtle sunrise element.
15. Desired Feeling/Message: Welcoming, energetic, high-quality, community-focused.
16. Inspiration/Examples: I like the clean look of Starbucks but want something less corporate, more indie coffee shop vibe."
